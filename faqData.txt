Q1: Why is cloud cost optimisation important?
A1: Cloud costs can grow rapidly due to over-provisioning, idle resources, and lack of visibility. Cloud cost optimisation ensures you pay only for what you use, maximises the return on your cloud investment, and frees up budget for innovation and growth.

Q2: How does CloudFirst identify cost-saving opportunities in my cloud?
A2: CloudFirst conducts a thorough cloud cost audit using tools like AWS Cost Explorer, Azure Cost Management, and Google Cloud Billing Insights. We identify idle resources, oversized instances, unattached storage, unused licenses, and opportunities for reserved capacity or savings plans.

Q3: What is right-sizing in cloud cost optimisation?
A3: Right-sizing is the process of matching the size and type of cloud resources (compute, memory, storage) to your actual workload requirements. By eliminating over-provisioned resources, right-sizing can significantly reduce your cloud bill without impacting performance.

Q4: Can CloudFirst help set up cloud budgets and cost alerts?
A4: Yes, CloudFirst helps configure cloud budgets, spending thresholds, and real-time cost anomaly alerts using native tools. This ensures your team is notified immediately if spending exceeds expected levels, preventing cost overruns.

Q5: How quickly can I see results from cloud cost optimisation?
A5: Quick wins like eliminating idle resources and right-sizing can show results within the first 30 days. Longer-term strategies such as reserved instance planning and architectural optimisation deliver sustained savings over 3–12 months.

Q1: What is Disaster Recovery on Cloud (DR on Cloud)?
A1: Disaster Recovery on Cloud is a strategy that uses cloud infrastructure to replicate and host your critical IT systems, so they can be quickly restored in the event of a disaster such as hardware failure, cyberattack, power outage, or natural calamity. It ensures business continuity with minimal downtime.

Q2: What is the difference between RTO and RPO in disaster recovery?
A2: RTO (Recovery Time Objective) is the maximum acceptable time to restore operations after a disaster. RPO (Recovery Point Objective) is the maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured in time. CloudFirst designs DR solutions to meet your specific RTO and RPO targets.

Q3: What disaster recovery options does CloudFirst offer?
A3: CloudFirst offers multiple DR strategies including Backup & Restore, Pilot Light, Warm Standby, and Multi-Site Active-Active, across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. The right approach is selected based on your business criticality, budget, and recovery time requirements.

Q4: How often does CloudFirst test disaster recovery plans?
A4: CloudFirst recommends and conducts regular DR drills — typically quarterly — to validate that recovery procedures work as expected. We document test results, identify gaps, and update the DR plan to ensure it remains effective and reliable.

Q5: Is cloud-based disaster recovery cost-effective compared to traditional DR?
A5: Yes, cloud-based DR is significantly more cost-effective than traditional disaster recovery, which requires maintaining a secondary physical data centre. With cloud DR, you pay only for storage and minimal compute during standby, with full compute capacity available only when a disaster occurs.
